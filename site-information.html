1. Who this applies to

This policy covers visitors, supporters who donate or tip, people who save a public note on the supporters leaderboard, and anyone who opens pages, forms, or links hosted on or linked from this site. 2. What I do not do for ordinary browsing

For normal reading and navigation of this website:

  • I do not require an account or login.

  • I do not build marketing profiles from ordinary page views on this site alone.

Your browser and hosting provider may still process technical connection data (such as IP address and request headers) as part of delivering the page. That is standard internet infrastructure, not a feature I use to profile you.

3. Information you may provide voluntarily

Some features only work if you choose to send information:

  • Display name — optional public name shown on the Top Supporters leaderboard (length limited on the form).
  • Note — optional public message shown with your supporter entry (length limited on the form).
  • Social / profile URL — if you attach one, it may be shown as a link next to your name on the leaderboard.
  • Donation amount — the amount you choose when tipping or donating.
  • Email and payment details — collected and processed by Stripe (or another payment provider you use, such as Venmo or Cash App) to complete the payment. I do not store full card numbers on this website.
  • Application details — if you apply via the Work For Me / Apply flow (for example through Tally or similar), that form provider receives whatever fields you submit.

Do not submit information you are not comfortable making public when a field is described as public (name, note, social URL).

4. How donations and the leaderboard work

When you use the on-site supporters / donate flow:

  1. Checkout is typically handled by Stripe. Stripe processes payment information under Stripe’s own privacy policy and terms.
  2. A backend service (currently hosted on Render) may receive payment confirmation (for example via webhook) and update supporter totals.
  3. Leaderboard data is stored in a database (currently Supabase). Public leaderboard reads are limited to fields needed for display — typically display name, note, total support amount, and social URL for entries with a positive total.
  4. Email and sensitive payment data are not exposed on the public leaderboard through the site’s public read path.

Tips or donations sent through Venmo, Cash App, or a Stripe Payment Link outside the on-site form may not automatically appear on the leaderboard unless they are matched and recorded separately.

5. Information stored in your browser

The site may use your browser’s localStorage (or similar local storage) for small, site-only preferences, for example:

  • Remembering a display name you previously used for the supporters form.
  • Remembering whether you liked or disliked the home comment UI.
  • Remembering whether you have already seen a brief splash or intro animation.

This data stays on your device unless you clear site data. It is not used to track you across other websites.

6. Third-party services and outbound links

This site links to and may load assets from third parties, including but not limited to:

  • Stripe (payments and tips)
  • Supabase (leaderboard database)
  • Render (API / backend for supporters)
  • GitHub / GitHub Pages or raw content (hosting, images, project links)
  • Beacons (courses and Capes++ shop)
  • Tally (applications)
  • Google Fonts and icon / favicon CDNs
  • Social and product sites: Instagram, Discord, GitHub, Spotify, Telegram, NameMC, GD Browser, Venmo, Cash App, and project domains such as freeuniversitytools.com and serverly.store

When you click those links or load those resources, the third party may collect data according to their own privacy practices. I am not responsible for their policies or practices.

7. How information is used

Information is used only as needed to:

  • Operate and display the website and supporters leaderboard.
  • Process tips and donations and acknowledge support.
  • Respond to applications or messages you send.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix technical issues.
  • Comply with law if legally required.

I do not sell personal information. I do not rent or trade supporter lists for advertising.

8. Public content and visibility

Anything you submit for public display (supporter name, note, social URL, and aggregate support amount) can be seen by anyone who views the leaderboard or related pages. Assume public fields are public worldwide. Do not include private contact details, passwords, or sensitive personal data in notes.

9. Retention

Leaderboard and donation-related records are kept while useful to operate the supporters feature and maintain accurate totals, unless you request deletion where feasible. Browser localStorage remains until you clear it. Payment providers retain records according to their own retention rules and legal obligations (for example tax and fraud prevention).

10. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect systems that store supporter data (access controls, restricted public database reads, secrets kept off the public site). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You use the site and payment providers at your own risk regarding residual security risk.

11. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not donate, tip, or submit personal information if you are under the applicable age without appropriate parental / guardian involvement as required by law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact me so it can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

12. International visitors

The site may be hosted and processed in the United States or other countries. If you access it from elsewhere, your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations, which may have different data-protection laws than your home country.

13. Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information I hold about you, or to object to certain processing. For public supporter entries, you can often update your note through the site tools when available. For other requests (for example removing a name from the leaderboard), contact me with enough detail to locate the entry. I may need to verify the request relates to you. Payment disputes and card data requests should go to Stripe or the payment method you used.

14. Do Not Track and similar signals

Because this site does not use advertising trackers for ordinary browsing, there is little additional action taken in response to browser “Do Not Track” signals beyond the practices already described here.
